About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Vijay Vazirani and Prof. Ioannis Panageas. My research focuses on approximation algorithms and algorithmic game theory.

I earned my BS in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics from Tufts University and my MS in Computer Science from Northwestern University. At Northwestern, I wrote my thesis on the approximation properties of Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) supervised by Prof. Konstantin Makarychev.
